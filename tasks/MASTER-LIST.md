## How to read status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Complete — bounded | A named slice has completion evidence. The surrounding product can remain unfinished. |
| Live — reported | A dated release/readback receipt reports deployment. This audit did not make a fresh production request. |
| Local — verified/review pending | Local implementation/check evidence exists; independent acceptance and release are stated separately. |
| Active / next | Work is underway per a current receipt, or is the next documented dependency; this is not a new dispatch. |
| Blocked | A concrete prerequisite, ownership handoff, evidence gap or action approval is missing. |
| Parked / tentative | Retained possibility, explicitly deferred or not adopted. |
| Historical — reconcile | Preserved legacy obligation without enough current evidence to claim open, complete or live. |

Owners below identify existing lanes or a role to assign. “Unassigned” is intentional. A lease labelled active is a reservation, not evidence that a worker is presently running.

5. At a new work session, read the relevant current row, latest owner receipt and source requirements. Select only authorized next work. When the task ends, record the outcome here through the custodian; closed slices stay searchable.
