The rules path had only four nonempty proposal sets, all held; its other six checks asked whether the source contained material news. Their high scores indicate source relevance, **not successful extraction or correct empty results**. These probabilities are uncalibrated, have no per-claim rationale, and are not independent corroboration. The practical next implementation boundary is a typed adapter: preserve governing clauses and antecedents, keep attendance/health/selection separate, encode projected versus confirmed categories, and bind replacement/correction references before any native action. Reuse the retained outputs as regressions. Keep irreducible ambiguity held. This comparison does not authorize production integration, dispatch or another paid experiment.
