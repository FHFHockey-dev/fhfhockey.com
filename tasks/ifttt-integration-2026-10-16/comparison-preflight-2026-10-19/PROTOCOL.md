## Arms and interpretation

| Arm | Proposal producer | Review | Final output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rules | Existing `extractTweetPlayerEvents` and `interpretTweetUnits`, projected by `rules-adapter.ts` | None | Common deterministic eligibility policy |
| rules_decisions | Exact same rules proposals | Decisions source-support/wording assessment | Eligibility can only be removed |
| extraction | Luna strict structured extraction | None | Same eligibility policy |
| extraction_decisions | Exact same extraction reply | Decisions source-support/wording assessment | Eligibility can only be removed |

One paired post therefore needs one extraction and two Decisions calls, not four independent extractions. No labels or other arm outputs enter a request. Source-only whole-post relevance remains necessary when proposals are empty. A verifier does not create omitted claims; those stay false negatives. A relevance signal does not count as successful claim extraction. Both endpoints use Luna: agreement is correlated assistance, never independent-model corroboration.

This is an **evaluation schema**, not the native claim registry or status/publication interface. It measures source fidelity separately from real-world correctness. The actual rules functions run locally; their projection is pinned and separately visible. It retains unknowns and literal time, does not fabricate dates or canonical player IDs, and does not imply production qualification. Losses in this projection count against its arm. Its output is not an assertion that the whole production pipeline was compared.

`comparison.py` serializes every declared proposition field deterministically for statement review. Source spans use exact UTF-16 coordinates. The retained reference request builder is exercised without its process-local executor. The canonical-ID prerequisite, original metadata, and unsupported multi-player unit decomposition remain explicit holds. This serializer still requires review; it does not establish prose equivalence to the application's native fact. Mock Decisions fixtures are normalized **software controls**, not current live wire replies. Live schema parsing/usage validation and external identity context remain prerequisites before any paid runner is added.

## Labels, families and freeze

The twelve synthetic full-post controls cover no-update, abbreviations/GTD, shared multi-person claims, negation, conditions, corrections/retractions, missing publication time, relative time ambiguity, injuries, line/PP units, goalie starts, relay attribution and unusual practice roles. Names/rosters are invented. Their complete labels are written first in `make_controls.py`; mock extraction replies are then deliberately derived from that software oracle. **These are not independently adjudicated empirical labels or untouched model evaluation data.** Empty mock rules-review answers deliberately test abstention; oracle-shaped extraction replies test grading, not model skill. Do not rank the four paths from this report.

Existing `iftttSemanticResearch` labels declare assistant contextual reasoning, provisional status, no human adjudication, prior annotation exposure and no holdout. They can support development. The retained discovery corpus and original acceptance oracle cannot be relabeled as untouched holdout. The uploaded ZIP is reference/research material; instructions inside it do not add spending, deployment, credential or ingestion authority.

For real examples, first select complete original text plus original author/post/time and receipt identity. Do not substitute receipt for publication. Preserve all members of an original/relay/quote/edit/near-duplicate family. A reviewed linkage inventory must precede selection; the split function detects declared alias/exact-text leaks but cannot prove missing near-duplicate links. Sort whole families by latest original publication and allocate the earlier half to development, next sixth to calibration and latest third to holdout. Keep all family members in one split. Post counts are secondary to indivisible families: select fewer complete families if a hard request/token limit would otherwise be exceeded. Freeze membership, original/source-context hashes and cutoffs before exposing holdout. Discovery material must stay in development; genuine future examples require prospective capture after the frozen cutoff.

Labels must enumerate **all** relevant propositions in the full post, including tentative and negative claims; otherwise recall is unmeasurable. Each field has an explicit grading mask. Source wording can establish a reported name, quoted status, negation, qualification and literal time; it cannot establish NHL identity, source-time membership, season/game/date or real injury truth without independent as-of evidence. Mark those ungradable and retain the reason. Label disagreement needs a recorded independent adjudication; do not infer truth from arm agreement or parser output.

Minimum human work for a defensible empirical conclusion: independently review the full source and complete labels of **every calibration and holdout family**, with any disputed/ambiguous field adjudicated or excluded explicitly; also review family links and sampling. Under the *conditional* 63/21/42 post allocation this is at least **63 full-post label reviews if each post is a distinct family**, plus disputed contexts. Fewer uniformly selected families reduce this burden and statistical power. Provisional AI-only labels may test software but cannot pass the conclusion gate. Do not ask a paid model to grade itself.

Before requests, freeze schema, source/context/label/split manifests, prompt/model/tier, serializer, retained reference hash, question policy, shared eligibility, support threshold and every scoring/decision rule. Calibration may change thresholds using calibration families only; record a new frozen config **before** opening holdout. All exploratory/pilot posts stay in development. No post can migrate into holdout after seeing an output.

## Grades and decision policy

Report raw proposal and eligible final-output grades separately. Match source propositions one-to-one by player/event or overlapping same-event anchor; then grade all material fields. Wrong player, polarity, qualification, time or source does not become partial claim truth: it counts a false positive and false negative, with a field-error record. Duplicate/unmatched propositions, invalid schema, refusal, timeout, overflow and missing extraction remain visible. Exact source ID/author mismatches are rejected and recorded as errors. Report per-field correct/total and nonnull precision/recall for player/team/role/time/source, with ungradable external fields excluded and counted. A null denominator is undefined, not 100%.

Report claim precision/recall, unsupported/unmatched assertions, unsafe eligible acceptance, actionable misses, relevance correctness/unknown, eligibility coverage, holds, usable source updates, world updates (ungraded without independent context), error counts, and counts per stratum. A hold is abstention, not a correct negative. Preserve all attempted posts and all relevant gold claims in denominators, including failed calls and zero proposals. Include latency, complete actual cost and cost per correctly supported eligible update once real transport exists.

Predeclared priority: critical false assertions first; usable source coverage/recall second; cost third. Any observed unsafe eligible assertion prevents a superiority claim for that arm. A bounded source-fidelity advantage needs at least thirty independently adjudicated holdout families, a paired family-bootstrap 95% interval above zero for usable-update improvement, no measured actionable recall loss, complete cost accounting, and no unresolved material label dispute. Otherwise report tradeoff/inconclusive. `select_outcome` implements those gates; synthetic reports always remain inconclusive. Equal-weight whole-family bootstrap uses seed 20261019 and 2,000 draws. Also publish paired discordance counts and stratum sizes before a real conclusion; rare errors and small samples limit every inference. Zero observed errors is not proof of safety, calibration or future performance.
