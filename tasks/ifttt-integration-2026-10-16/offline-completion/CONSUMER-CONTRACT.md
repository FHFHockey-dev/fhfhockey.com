## Concrete future consumer adapter requirements

Reuse the existing registry/outbox/lease mechanisms. No new queue or bespoke delivery service is proposed. The specific sink adapter must:

1. Re-read the complete current qualification/global-dispute/history decision under transactional fencing before acting. Clearing `requires_review` alone is insufficient. A material correction or unproved old effect stays held.
2. Accept an immutable stable action key plus exact sink natural-key/payload mapping, original applicability and first admission clocks. Reject mismatched same-key payloads; preserve the original mapping/bytes.
3. Commit the exact sink effect and authoritative receipt atomically where that sink permits it, or document the bounded partial states when it does not. Unknown response/expired lease enters reconciliation; it never proves absence.
4. Reconcile every required parent, child, trigger/queue result and legacy receipt without mutation. Return applied only on full exact readback; return positively not-applied only on complete absence/fence proof. Otherwise remain uncertain/held. Re-enqueue helpers are not readback APIs.
5. Use existing fenced retry ceilings; do not automatically send a second action for the same uncertain effect. Keep canonical reservations and original expiry/cutoff. No exactly-once external-effects promise.

Offline work available without a hosted read includes dedicated fixtures for these adapters, explicit payload/membership mismatch and fan-out/ack-loss tests, and pure 52-case projections. Editing forecast/line/news consumer writers requires coordination with their owners; no such writer was authorized or changed here. Authentic absence/retention/fencing and actual deployed receipt reuse require separate bounded evidence. The catalog package alone cannot supply them.
