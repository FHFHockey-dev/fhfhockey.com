# Source adjudication protocol v1

Lexical scores are diagnostics of the frozen contract only. They are **not factual-error counts, semantic equivalence scores, verifier benefit or a factual-quality ranking**. No automatic factual-quality winner is permitted, even when a lexical score is perfect. The historical development benchmark and earlier lexical reports remain unchanged.

Review the public sheet without consulting its private mapping, producer names, benchmark answers, verifier probabilities or lexical scores. Neutral arm and stable proposal IDs conceal producer identity; original producer-specific claim IDs are replaced only for display and preserved in the private mapping/raw outputs. Raw and aligned variants remain separately identified. Identical proposals can be displayed once with all neutral appearances; retain the union of every asserted field and every revision. No raw artifact is rewritten.

For **every** proposal—not only automatic mismatches—read the complete source, exact evidence and available context. Mark equivalent/source-supported, factual-or-qualification error, or unresolved. Quote the source clause and explain briefly. Separately mark duration/numeric range, negation, uncertainty/conditions, subject/reference identity, role/eligibility, drill context, correction/retraction, unproved binding, or other errors. In particular:

- Compare decimal endpoints, ranges, approximations and relative-time wording directly; punctuation-normalized equality is not proof.
- An equivalent wording can be source-supported despite a lexical mismatch. Do not change the raw producer output or tune the prompt to make it match.
- Inspect every added role, team, ID, season/game/time or medical assertion even when ignored by the old matcher.
- Inspect pronoun and drill references using the full thread; a shared word is insufficient subject identity.
- Preserve every qualifier, conditional and negation. Attendance, selection, health and practice role remain distinct.
- Review corrections against predecessors; retain immutable evidence while retracting/superseding only an unsupported interpretation. A quotation alone is not a correction.

After reviewing proposals, list missing source-supported updates for each family and judge the producer's whole set as supported, unsupported, unresolved or missing. This is separate from lexical TP/FP/FN. Keep all ten groups and the frozen 50-unit denominator; carry unresolved judgments and missing output separately. All model arms are currently missing/not run; the populated sheet contains existing local rules outputs only, never fabricated model responses.

Only after the human review is frozen with a hash may its private arm mapping be opened. Report lexical diagnostics and source-adjudicated judgments in independent columns, with both denominators and unresolved counts. Never infer a human result from an automatic score. Whole-set verifier probability/refusal/missing and its explicitly human-adjudicated correctness get separate columns; it is not calibrated per-claim confidence, independent corroboration, extraction repair or native eligibility. Do not call an LLM judge or tune prompts/labels after unblinding. No automatic winner; any later human conclusion must retain small-sample/exposure limitations.

Independent real-world truth and owner publication readiness remain ungraded unless separately supplied. This sheet does not authenticate player/roster/schedule context, authorize ingestion/publication or permit provider calls.
