**How uncertainty is handled.** The source can establish what TJ decided or what a reporter recorded. A historical screenshot, provider-side outcome, permission, live account state or current deployment is only treated as independently established here when the evidence below says so. Unverified claims remain dated or open in the crosswalk. No completed child waives the parent's remaining user-facing acceptance.
