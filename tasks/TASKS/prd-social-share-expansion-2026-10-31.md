# FHFH Social Share expansion: plan and requirements

Status: **planning only; parent-task review pending**. Owner: Claude implementation; independent Astra audit. Date: 2026-10-31. Player Cards is a separate Codex/Astra lane. This plan extends the existing Social Share suite and preserves Monday/Game Grid and Tuesday Trends work.

## Outcome and concise plan

Build a flexible library of useful skater and goalie posts with evidence that survives a share export. Start by reconciling owners and shared definitions, then implement independent skater and goalie modules against qualified snapshots. Reuse Game Grid, Tuesday Trends, GoalieShareChart, TGV and Start Chart instead of replacing them. Integrate through one Social Share writer, verify local browser/export behavior and freeze a candidate for independent Astra audit.

Thursday/Friday BSH and goalie Category Rescue are useful editorial options, not a mandatory calendar or automated posting schedule. Weekly and rest-of-week selections must represent their actual selected dates. Ratio Impact remains a named longer-term task; sentiment/Gemini discovery remains prospective until its source and capability are established.

The [parent-task list](../tasks-prd-social-share-expansion-2026-10-31.md) requires confirmation before detailed subtask generation. This packet authorizes no implementation, source collection, posting or deployment.

## Current evidence and reuse boundaries

- Existing suite: `web/components/SocialShare/SocialShare.tsx`, `web/lib/socialShare/*`, `/social-share/[format]`, its API route and `web/e2e/social-share.spec.ts`. Formats include game-grid, tuesday-trends, remaining-week, player-spotlight and category-rescue; a format's presence does not establish qualified live inputs.
- Tuesday Trends already has production, ownership, deployment and rate sections with source-state and per-game reconstruction logic. Retain its qualified zero/gap semantics; inspect the current owner and runtime path before extending L5 to L10/STD.
- Monday matchups/targets use Game Grid metrics, a top-16 WeekScore eligibility rule for streaming nights and qualified player picks. `targets.ts` currently has a fixed `<50%` ownership ceiling and up to three targeted skaters; that is not the requested dynamic Off-Night Edge threshold or one F plus one D per team.
- `GoalieShareChart` and `goalieWorkload.ts` exist. The chart's pie uses **appearance share including relief**. The helper has official starts only when each recorded team game has exactly one labeled starter; recorded rows alone do not prove a complete completed-team-game schedule. Crease Shift needs schedule reconciliation and a start-share denominator.
- TGV lives in `web/pages/trueGoalieValue.tsx` and `web/components/GoaliePage/goalieCalculations.ts`. Current weekly eligibility is at least one start and 1,200 TOI seconds. `calculateWeeklyRanking` compares selected categories with league weekly averages, counts **ties as better** (`>=` / `<=`), skips null/NaN/zero league-average values, and classifies the percentage of comparable categories as Elite ≥80, Good ≥60, Average ≥45, Bad ≥30, else Abysmal. Weekly grade points are 20/10/5/3/1; `calculateGoalieRankings` counts grades and sorts summed weekly points. Game-to-game variance uses configurable fantasy points. These are inspected implementation facts, **not newly approved thresholds**, and not the requested independent counts above/below each category average.
- Start Chart already separates confirmation/participation, source coverage and conditional forecast basis. Reuse its admitted contract. GSAx and workload signals require their own source, strength and exposure rather than replacing TGV or asserting goalie skill from shot volume.

## Decisions and gates

Parent approval comes first under generation rule step 4: this is unusually large, and TGV semantics, incomplete sources and shared ownership affect the decomposition. After approval, generate actionable subtasks for **all** parents, including prospective and longer-term items. Keep discovery, fixture behavior, real-input admission and activation separate.

TGV per-category counts require a definition checkpoint; the inspected historical thresholds are not defaults for the requested feature. Dynamic ownership defaults, Nightly Floor minimum GP/coverage policy, cohort eligibility and comparison periods need documented choices at their tasks. Recommend configurable values with visible active settings, not hidden hard-coded editorial rules. GSAx/chance source qualification and sentiment discovery can be blocked independently while local modules continue.
