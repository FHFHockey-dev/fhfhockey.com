## Concrete recommendation prepared for final handoff

Keep IFTTT as recall-oriented discovery. Require a validated, versioned `goalie-evidence:1` before precision-sensitive starter selection. Hashtags, mentions, account route, `goalie_start` classification and goalie list order do not establish who starts which game.

Proposed structured fields:

- Identity/provenance: `claimId`, `contractVersion`, `parserVersion`, `rawPayloadHash`, `postId` (string), `canonicalUrl`, `originalPostId`, `originalAuthorId`/`originalHandle`, separate relay identity; relation `original|quote|repost|reply|edit|unknown`, `referencedPostIds`, `editHistoryIds`, source text hash. Unavailable fields stay null with review reasons.
- Authority: verified registry source ID/version, `club|attributed_coach|credentialed_beat|aggregator|unknown`, covered teams, effective dates, attribution/evidence span; server-computed priority and independent-origin key. Club announcement/verified coach attribution can support confirmation; beat writers can report explicit confirmation or observations; aggregators inherit verified original evidence, and reposts add no votes. Newer lower-authority posts cannot silently overrule official applicable evidence.
- Times: `originalPostedUtc`, `relayPostedUtc`, `ingestedUtc`, `interpretedUtc`, raw timestamp, precision/time basis, explicit IANA source/reference timezone; never infer original time from receipt time.
- Identity/binding: stable `nhlPlayerId`, raw name, resolution basis, event-time membership and alias approval evidence/revisions; `teamId`, `gameId`, `opponentId`, `seasonId`, `scheduledStartUtc`, `scheduleRevision`; reference date `explicit|relative_resolved|inferred|unknown`, timezone and per-claim binding spans/status. Resolve tonight/tomorrow from original publication in known reference timezone against team/opponent/schedule; unresolved/B2B/rescheduled claims remain visible.
- Semantics: `claimType=confirmed_starter|expected|likely|first_off|warmup_starter|backup|out|retraction`; exact source span offsets/context, `modality=affirmative|tentative|negated|conditional|question`, claim subject/attributed speaker, explicit starter evidence. Distinguish observed warmup starter from merely skating or leading the team out.
- Lifecycle: `accepted`, review reasons, validity/expiry/recheck time, `supersedesClaimIds`, `retractsClaimIds`, authorized target relation and reason; preserve all revisions and availability cutoffs.

State machine: raw → extracted → unresolved/review or game-bound → expected/likely/confirmed/warmup-verified; applicable incompatible claims → conflicted; authorized target-specific correction/retraction → superseded/retracted plus recomputation; expiry/start/reschedule → unavailable/closed. Do not make confidence monotonically increase. Retraction removes evidence, not proof of the other goalie. Same-author updates must match game/scope and original author; handle matching alone is insufficient. Independent contradictions do not cease being conflicts after ten minutes. Preserve prior issued/frozen decisions; late evidence cannot rewrite what was available at an earlier cutoff.

Probabilities remain separate from claim status: no new likely=.8/first-off=.9 maps. Existing confirmed=1 is a planning point-mass assumption, not proof of zero late-change risk; expose that truth and recheck near warmups/lineup lock. Unknown/conflict means unavailable start probability or explicitly labeled model-only scenarios, not automatic 50/50. Retain residual mass, avoid double weighting, distinguish expected from if-starting stats, preserve zero-relief limitation, and do not count confirmation as realized minimum credit. Category recommendations require weighted raw SV%/GAA components, negative GA consequences and minimum-forfeiture risk; no calibrated win/risk probability claims.

Smallest near-term work: close affirmative/modality gate and span scope; add original identity/time/authority and game-bound evidence gate before sourceObservations; reconcile authorized corrections/exclusions and remove ten-minute conflict expiry; propagate status/source link/posted time/recheck/revision into both consumers. Reuse `web/lib/lines/types.ts:77` (strict instants), `binding.ts:11`, `identity.ts`, `producerContract.ts:27`, `reconcile.ts:20`, `targetLookup.ts` patterns without forcing goalie semantics into ES/PP selectors. Keep existing atomic capture, job queue, shared consumer admission and revision invalidation. Defer learned source weights, multilingual/model extraction and calibrated goalie/category joint distributions until labeled data exists. Verify schema/code/flags parity before any separately authorized rollout.
