**Release decision.** Keep the existing writer until the external queue/export owner supplies a reader inventory or the actual cursor configuration covering those consumers. If any reader requires last delivery, keep its existing signal during transition. Introduce a separately reviewed delivery receipt keyed by a stable delivery/run ID and dedupe key, with database-recorded delivery time. Record the receipt for unchanged deliveries and real corrections. During overlap preserve the legacy heartbeat and the verdict protection, verify the consumer sees natural repeats, corrections and retries on both paths, then move its checkpoint with an agreed overlap and deduplication window. Establish how the consumer handles late commits before adopting a timestamp cursor. Historical delivery times cannot be reconstructed from the latest edit time. That transition requires a different candidate.
