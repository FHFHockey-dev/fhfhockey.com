# BLSH first-pass readiness

November 7, 2026. Local candidate and read-only source audit; no release authorization or production change.

## Goal and settled scope

Help a manager distinguish a rebound/cooling candidate from an opportunity change, investigate the evidence, save a watchlist and compare a manager-entered trade. Keep retrospective BLSH evidence, forward FORGE production and observed market trades separate. Include F/D lists and a VuDu goalie view; default trade horizon is 10 scheduled team games, with 5 and 20 options.

The November 3 owner decisions supersede the older equal-track headline and generic forecast requirements:

- Lead with actual league points, FHFH Standard by default; NHL points and Game Score are supporting tracks.
- Use eligible top 25 per F/D group, with full lists available. Withhold insufficient history, short windows and irreconcilable evidence rather than filling the lists.
- Personal history uses a provisional recency-weighted baseline; approximately 20 games and chance screens remain provisional. TJ already chose current-season weight 1.0 after development testing, frozen for the reserve test. That choice is not a claim of untouched validation or runtime adoption.
- Opportunity-adjusted context may be unavailable. It must not silently change the underlying score or rank.
- Forecast availability does not block the core lists. A missing forecast can leave a clearly labeled retrospective trade comparison, not a zero forecast.
- Yahoo market evidence is a priority, but automated market sourcing is not a first-release prerequisite. A manual offer is not an observed price or proof of acceptance.

Sources: `tasks/TASKS/prd-buy-low-sell-high.md`; `tasks/artifacts/blsh-brief-decisions-2026-11-03/DECISIONS.md`. Poolforge is useful display inspiration, not validation of these thresholds or methods.

## Decisions and blockers, without expanding scope

- **Immediate evidence blocker:** retained native receipt or explicit non-retention, followed by a reviewed capture boundary if needed. No tool action was denied and no collection is pending with an uncertain outcome.
- **Window membership/admission contract:** source-observation and captured-window freshness diagnostics are complete, without changing v1 admission. Independently established complete latest-five appearance membership is still missing. Fresh receipts alone cannot prove a captured subset is a complete current window; no threshold, real acceptance or current-use policy has been widened.
- **Method decision:** how the operational personal baseline is admitted while 2025–26 remains reserved; a development win is not permission to claim validation. The original baseline/threshold choices stay provisional.
- **Goalie input/method gap:** complete current-season team-game cohort and retained observations, plus admitted forecasts where available; distinguish descriptive observed/forward views from the unaccepted retrospective goalie gap. Current custody must be coordinated before shared producer changes, not inferred from old diary ownership.
- **Release decision:** approve one reviewed candidate and explicit deployment scope only after the above acceptance work. No production release is included in the current authorization.
- **Not added as first-pass blockers:** automated Yahoo ingestion, a composite headline, calibrated recovery probabilities, optional opportunity adjustment, account-backed sync or a second model. Missing qualified forecasts do not block the skater lists.
