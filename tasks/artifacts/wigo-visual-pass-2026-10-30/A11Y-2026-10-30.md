WCAG 2.1 AA, checked by hand on the fictional fixture with `tools/a11y.cjs` and `tools/popovers.cjs`, after steps 1–9. Axe is parked until Monday, so this is not an automated scan. No screen reader was run: the screen-reader notes come from reading the DOM.

## What passes

- **Keyboard order (1280×800):** search → Clear → pace help → ratings help → drivers help → compare selects → compare help → 35 metric buttons → game log averages → chart Reset and view buttons → game score help → strength pills → slider. It follows the page.
- **Focus:** every tab stop has a visible style. Selects gained a working focus ring in step 4, help summaries in step 2.
- **Names:** no control is unnamed. ↺ is named "Reset".
- **Contrast:** everything else in the rendered text passes 4.5:1, including DIFF colours and rating values.
- **Colour alone:** DIFF has a sign, L and R columns have letter markers, active averages are bold, the selected metric is bold and has `aria-pressed`.
- **Zoom:** 200% of a 1280 window is 640 px wide, which uses the phone layout.

## Accepted, not proposed for change

Desktop targets are small on purpose: metric rows are 65×13 px at 1280, because 36 metrics must fit one screen (TJ's no-scroll decision, pinned by e2e). The whole row label is the target, and the phone layout has larger ones.
