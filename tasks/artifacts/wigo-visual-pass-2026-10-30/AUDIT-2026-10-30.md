## What already works

- The matrix is clearly the centre of the page, with the 36 metrics in two halves readable at 1280.
- Red and green DIFF values carry a sign and a bar, so colour is never the only signal.
- Everything fits one screen at 1280×800, with an 11 px floor.
- The team-colour rail on identity is the single brand accent, apart from the Category percentiles band.

## Constraints for any change

- **Behaviour:** every chart value, rating, percentile, query and data behaviour stays the same.
- **Layout:** no scrolling at 1280×800, 1440×900 and 1920×1080; the 1200 px / 700 px fit-or-flow switch stays; the 768 px phone tabs stay; 90% max width, centred (`$page-max-width`). `web/e2e/wigo-one-page.spec.ts` pins these, plus the 11 px floor, no clipped cells, and the matrix's 9 columns × 18 rows.
- **Text pinned by tests:** "Player GP: … · Requested … · Applied …", the threshold sentences, and the matrix headers ("Totals", "STDL", …). Where wording moves, the words stay in the DOM.
- **Files:** only the lease `WIGO-UI-PASS` paths. `components/CategoryCoverageChart*` (the radar's chart body) is outside the lease; its title is in `WigoDashboardSections.tsx`, which is inside it.

## Proposed step order

Each step is one commit, with before/after screenshots and an approve/edit/revert pop-up.

1. **Phone repairs.** Percentiles chart height, clipped titles and notes on Trends and Comparison, oversized legends. It goes first because one tab is unusable today.
2. **One panel title style.** ⓘ only (no ▶ anywhere), no filled band, a calmer COMPARE, one season format.
3. **Top bar.** Stop repeating the name, remove the decorative label, quiet the tagline.
4. **Compare toolbar and matrix.** Lighter column tint, shorter caption, lighter labels.
5. **Chart row.** Legend and Reset on the title line, one line of averages, a small "Game mean" label. More room for the charts.
6. **Identity and production.** One row of numbers, no repeats.
7. **Ratings, drivers, schedule.** One header for ratings, flat driver cards, quieter schedule rows.
8. **Rate percentiles.** One control line, detail in the tooltip, fix "10 0".
9. **Type, colour and radius scale.** Same approach as RSO step 5, including the phone's 11 px floor.
10. **Review.** Accessibility review plus a design critique and copy review of the finished page. Anything found becomes its own approve/edit step.
