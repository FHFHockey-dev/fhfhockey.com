## Confirmed product decisions

- Two distinct end pages: The Sko Element and Buy Low / Sell High.
- The Sko Element includes skaters and VuDu goalies. F/D reference classes are separate, especially IPP.
- Role/opportunity changes are part of sustainability. Show persistence/reversal and volatility without arbitrary penalties or disqualifying promotions.
- Default next 10 scheduled team games, selectable 5 or 20; do not confuse that with player appearances or a fantasy matchup week.
- Initial market page: rebound/cooling watchlists plus manager-entered trade comparisons. A market-value gap needs a comparable explicit market reference; automated market sourcing is not a release prerequisite.
- Reuse FORGE forecasts and a single interpretation contract. Prefer a clean interpretation core and selected proven plumbing over reviving incompatible old formulas.
- RSO/FORGE work continues in parallel, as explicitly confirmed by TJ. Current FORGE/RSO/Start Chart release work retains priority; independent SKO work must avoid file/resource collisions, not wait for a public release. Exact routes, source availability, display scales and predictive thresholds are not fabricated as settled facts.
