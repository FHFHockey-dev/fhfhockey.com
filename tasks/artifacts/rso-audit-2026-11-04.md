## Runtime trace and supported objectives

`pages/roster-schedule-optimizer.tsx` mounts `components/RosterScheduleOptimizer/RosterScheduleOptimizer.tsx`. That component retains workspace intent separately from loaded shared/provider state, uses principal/context/generation checks on refresh, and calls `hooks/useRosterPlanning.ts`. The hook cancels obsolete requests and hides obsolete results; `hooks/rosterPlanningExecution.ts` and `lib/rosterScheduleOptimizer/executionAdapter.ts` coordinate the worker/current-read path. A result is not made current merely because its worker completed.

Shared data enters through `pages/api/v1/roster-schedule-optimizer/data.ts` and `schedule.ts`, then `lib/rosterScheduleData/planning.ts` / `planningInputs.ts`. Bounded reads check pagination identity/count/completeness. Games retain game IDs, team sides, timestamps, statuses and schedule revisions. Provider data enters through authenticated `provider.ts` / `yahoo-current-data.ts` and `lib/integrations/yahoo/rosterPlanning.ts`, including identity resolution, current league eligibility, ownership, reserve placement, reported rules and provenance. Fantrax authoritative in-season planning explicitly returns unsupported. Public ownership percentage is not treated as league availability.

`currentPlanningRead.ts`, `privatePlanningRead.ts`, `refreshForecastInputs.ts`, and `yahooEligibilityCache.ts` preserve source-specific authority/freshness; `resolvePlanningContributions` admits qualified forecasts, with target-use permissions, issuance context, revisions and participation scope. No forecast policy was loosened.

`planning.ts` evaluates a no-move roster and full proposed trajectories through `planningTimeline.ts`. Assignment uses `lib/projections/weightedAssignment.ts`, an exact rectangular weighted matcher with empty choices. The older `matching.ts` maximizes count first; it is not the active points-planning path and was left unchanged. Candidate metrics re-evaluate complete swaps; search and `streamingChain.ts` examine complete trajectories with pruning/budget disclosures. `candidatePlanMetrics.ts`, `contributionLedger.ts`, `benchDecisions.ts`, and the candidate/itinerary/evidence components expose accounting and exclusions.

Supported objectives are daily/verified-weekly legal schedule capacity (`agp`), qualified expected points (`outcome`), and category component comparisons. Category results are provisional mean comparisons with incomparable tradeoffs retained, not scalar player quality or calibrated whole-matchup win probability. A points coverage preference is a distinct manager-selected constraint. Weekly mode requires independent lineup periods/locks; acquisition periods do not substitute for them. Season-long points/roto are now explicitly rejected by the Yahoo RSO adapter.
