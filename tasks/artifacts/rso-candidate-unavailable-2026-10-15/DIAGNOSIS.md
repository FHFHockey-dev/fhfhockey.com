It evaluates at most 40 candidates sorted by stable ID; display count is not evaluation scope. Unevaluated candidates remain discovery rows.
3. The generator/evaluator must retain a legal plan with `budgetVerified` (`candidatePlanMetrics.ts:63-64`). Unknown effective timing, unresolved waiver clearance, missing period coverage, unknown remaining allowance/cost/waiver budget, unavailable drops, protected players, locks and capacity can prevent a retained plan. `planningTimeline.ts:13-22, 77-150` establishes those constraints. Legal baseline also requires resolved roster/capacity/reserve placements and weekly windows/locks (`planning.ts:748`).
4. Even with retained plans, `scheduleKnown = baseline.legal && !snapshot.rules.unsupported.length && snapshot.evidence.schedule?.completeness === "complete"` (`candidatePlanMetrics.ts:62`). Otherwise every retained plan's net AGP is null. This gate does **not** require forecasts. Net AGP is the legal plan's active-game count minus the optimized original roster's active-game count, including sacrifices/reshuffling; gross upcoming NHL games are insufficient.

## Why alphabetical order appears

`CandidateBrowser.tsx:55-68` joins worker comparisons back to the broader merged display catalog. No comparison or no retained plan yields null metrics. Known points sort first descending; without points, known net AGP sorts descending. Rows with both null are separated into **unranked players**, ordered by verified future canonical team-game count when available, then `name.localeCompare` and stable player ID (`CandidateBrowser.tsx:30-33,111-127`). For team counts to be available, the canonical calendar must be complete, NHL team identity known, all relevant game clocks/states present, and game starts strictly later than the current discovery clock. Equal or unknown counts therefore yield alphabetical browsing under either net sort. This is not a highest-projection ranking and must not be presented as one.
