# Sko skater-points game and episode history v1

The 2024–25 regular-season development ledger has **47,224 played appearances, 920 skaters and 1,312 official game IDs**. Hot accounts for 2,401 appearances in 890 episodes, cold for 2,065 in 711 episodes. Another 23,692 are neutral and 19,066 have insufficient history for a confirmed state. The original pilot is preserved in the parent directory.

## Identity and coverage

Use the pinned WGO and games exports from the November 4 repeatable-read development capture at `~/Documents/Claude/2026-11-04/blsh-baseline/capture-20242025/`. The [development source report](../../blsh-baseline-2026-11-04/REPORT.md) records its scope. The [WGO reader/writer](../../../../web/pages/api/v1/db/update-wgo-skaters.ts) sources game-level skater summary points from the official NHL statistics API. The historical export is a retained derivative. The build checks its hashes and independently checks one player's six observations against the [archived official NHL regular-season log](../../wigo-legacy-identity-repair-2026-11-07/source/8476425-20242025.json).

* Each unique skater/season/game has GP=1 and positive TOI. Each game matches the official season, regular-season type and date in the retained games table. Each has two distinct clubs and each club has 82 distinct games. The other club on the same game ID supplies the opponent.
* All 47,224 points values equal goals plus assists. Primary and secondary assists sum to the official assist count. The original pilot's 35,246 player-season-dates each identify exactly one recorded appearance. Every overlapping points/goals/shots/TOI value agrees. The complete ledger adds 11,978 appearances including 197 additional players.
* Each of the 549 pilot ordinal resets occurred between consecutive official-ID appearances, always restarting at 15. No consecutive pair of retained pilot rows skipped an intervening WGO appearance. For Alex DeBrincat, Jan 30 game 2024020819 is appearance 51 and Feb 1 game 2024020834 is appearance 52, stored as 15. The pilot's reset interrupted its rolling history. Forward labels and the chart follow played games. [Reset crosswalk](ordinal-resets.csv)
* Team/position come from the appearance itself. There are 107 observed team changes. Ordinary trades preserve the player's scoring history. Internal same-club team dates without a skater row are recorded as “no recorded appearance.” The reason could be an injury, scratch, minor-league assignment or other absence. Across a trade, intermediate team dates are attached to the two possible clubs as affiliation unknown. No zero-valued appearance, DNP reason, line assignment or trade-effective timestamp is inferred. Team dates before first and after last appearances have no established service interval. [Team-date gaps](team-game-gaps.csv.gz)
* 2,616 team-game sheets contain 18 skater appearances, eight contain 17. A 17-skater sheet could be legitimate short-handed dressing. When one falls between a player's observed appearances for a plausible club and the player was absent, the next appearance starts a fresh segment. This gives 19 conservative boundaries. It does not turn those absences into missed games or claimed DNPs. The NST cross-check found 128 additional individual records on regular-season dates under two IDs entirely outside the WGO cohort. These lack an authoritative WGO appearance match and are excluded. A complete scratch/transaction register and individual NHL boxscores for every skater were unavailable.

## State and phase

The outcome key is all-strength skater NHL points (one per goal or assist). The versioned episode ID includes the policy, player, season, direction and first observable confirmed game ID. A `boundary-` key flags a left-censored identification. One ID spans consecutive closes in that confirmed state, even through a team change if the played history is continuous.

The first qualifying trailing-five minus previous non-overlapping 20 gap at ±0.40 points/GP is a breach. At the second consecutive same-side qualifying close the band begins, with no backdating. Last-five form becomes available on GP5, the personal reference and raw flag on GP25, and the two-close confirmed classification on GP26. Each suffix of history is invisible when constructing an earlier chart row. `breach` includes unsuccessful breaches. `confirmation` marks the first close where the current run is confirmed, and `exit` is the first outside close. State changes are knowable after the observed game's close using that prefix; the retained files cannot prove when providers first published their exact values.

Two cold runs are already active at the first classifiable close following an uncertain boundary. Their first confirmable closes have game IDs, their true starts and first breaches stay unknown, and their start-aligned buckets are excluded. The 76 hot and 37 cold runs still active at their last measurable close have no observed exits. A last-observed inside game is available for each episode, including those without a verified final inside game. [Episodes](episodes.csv)

For each observed start S the lead-in covers S−5…S−1. Opening covers S…S+4 and stops at the last inside close. During contains each observed inside close, and interior excludes the first observable inside close. For each observed exit E, the final inside and first outside closes have separate memberships. The exit window includes E−2…E+2 while available in the same segment. In a one-game streak E−2 can precede the episode. A game can occupy several descriptive buckets, or the exit and lead-in windows of two episodes. Model rows come directly from unique played appearances, never a phase join. ## Process availability

The [feature dictionary](feature-availability.csv) gives grain, strength, units, capture time and row support. [Process rows](process.csv.gz) have the same one-to-one identity as the [game ledger](games.csv.gz). All inputs are retrospective. The original parquet was materialized on Sep 26, 2025. The full WGO/NST development capture was taken Nov 4, 2026. Game-close publication and revision timestamps are unavailable.

* Total, EV and PP TOI are seconds. The available PP share is the player's PP seconds divided by their total seconds. Team PP opportunities, PP-unit assignment, linemates and effective-dated deployment decisions are unavailable here. Position and club are observed at game level. Calendar spacing between prior appearances describes realized rest, which can include time out of the lineup.
* WGO supplies 47,224 observations for shots, goals, assists, primary/secondary assists and TOI. The pilot adds missed shots for 35,246. Individual NST ixG and exposure require unique player/season/date, GP=1 and exact TOI, shots and goals agreement with WGO. 47,135 individual NST records join and 47,003 pass. The exposure-based rate uses aggregate ixG per 3,600 matched seconds.
* Individual attempts and high-danger chances come from the retained pilot's all-strength individual NST counts. Require the same reconciled individual record plus matching pilot TOI and ixG. This admits 35,087 rows. Six overlapping pilot-versus-capture ixG values differ at more than 0.0000001, direct evidence of source-version differences. On-ice GF/SF use separately retained all-strength on-ice counts with unique on-ice GP=1 and matched TOI, supporting 35,116 rows. On-ice shooting percentage uses goals and shots for while on the ice. Individual and on-ice chances have separate source mappings. [NST game-log field mapping](../../../../web/pages/api/v1/db/nst_gamelog_scraper.py)
* Nulls survive joins and censor feature windows. Individual shooting, goal-minus-ixG and assists are descriptive production/conversion measures at the issue close. They share ingredients with scoring labels and require a scoring-only baseline. A forward GP window may include only games strictly after issue. No stored labels, model outputs, protected blocks or later outcomes were read to generate these datasets.
