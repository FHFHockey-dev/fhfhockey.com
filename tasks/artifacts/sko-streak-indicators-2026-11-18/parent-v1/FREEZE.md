# Streak study v1 decision contract

Frozen on 2026-11-18 before fitting or scoring challengers. TJ's original pilot results and 2024–25 date/state availability were known. This is a retrospective development comparison. Each prediction is issued after the index appearance's final box score, before any subsequent appearance. Historical source availability is a separate check from mathematical prefix safety. The primary unit is one eligible NHL skater appearance per direction and decision. No score is for public serving.

## Outcomes and risk sets

Use all-strength NHL points, one per goal or assist, per played regular-season appearance. For the chart and comparable benchmark use the trailing five played games through t against the immediately preceding 20. A signed difference of at least 0.40 points/GP is a breach. Two consecutive same-side qualifying closes confirm. The confirmed interval starts at S, the confirming close, and ends before the first outside close E. Never backdate the confirmed band. Identity resolution, breaks and missingness are documented with the data. A uses the exact pilot's segmentation and state availability. B uses verified player-game continuity. They are different populations.

| Study | Decision at close t | Label |
|---|---|---|
| A, same-label | Hot/cold early onset while confirmed neutral and raw flag neutral | First raw breach at t+1, confirmed at t+2, with both subsequent states known. |
| A, same-label | Continue/exit while hot or cold with a known next state | Still on that side at t+1 / first outside at t+1. |
| B, forward | Hot/cold onset from confirmed neutral and unbreached at issue | Next five played games' mean exceeds/falls below the fixed personal reference visible at t by at least 0.40. This is a future production block, which need not contain a chart confirmation. |
| B, forward | Sustain/exit from an already-confirmed side | Same-side five-game pace criterion on t+1…t+5 / its complement. Exit here means failure to sustain the pace over the five-game horizon. A separately measures exact chart exits. |

For B the fixed reference is the chart's 20-game personal reference at issue (t−24…t−5). Freeze it to avoid a moving target. Require all five outcomes after t in the same verified appearance segment. Different index games may overlap in targets within a split. Phase memberships never supply duplicate model rows. No cross-season lookahead. Run both directions separately. Incomplete follow-up is unknown. Do not train on a right-censored negative.

## Chronology and candidate choice

Train on issue dates before 2025-03-01 and require the final target close strictly before that date. Development validation issues start 2025-03-01 and end with complete observed follow-up. Apply the split to singles, baselines and challenger identically. Use the same paired source support for comparisons and report coverage/attrition. Any pilot-wide aggregate replay is clearly distinguished from validation. No search over date, target, family subset, threshold, regularization or nonlinear model using validation outcomes.

Use logistic regression with standardized features, L2 C=1 and training-only preprocessing; no class reweighting. Fit prevalence, scoring level/distance (include level of the reference), persistence/episode age, then outgoing contribution to the five-game window. Compare the full process model to the scoring ladder incl. outgoing contribution. Use the full ladder as preselected comparator, also disclose a simpler baseline winning on validation. If an entire stage is constant or collinear, disclose this. Pilot-direction singles include shots and individual xG alongside original contrasts. Freeze the primary challenger as all admissible families plus the full baseline. Assess family contributions by dropping one family at a time. Missing features cannot be inferred from later corrections. Use training-only imputation and missingness indicators for partial optional process support, or restrict to common support for literal single-indicator comparisons and disclose denominators. Do not optimize subsets or swap a winner into the primary table.

Bounded families: opportunity (total, EV, PP minutes, share), generation (shots, attempts, individual xG, exposure-aware individual xG/60, dangerous chances), conversion (goals minus compatible xG, regressed personal shooting%, primary/secondary assists, compatible on-ice finishing), and known schedule/deployment context where lineage supports it. Compare trailing level, deviation from preceding reference and recent direction. A dated change in usage is an observed proxy; lineup changes need effective timestamps. Withhold incompatible or unverifiable measures. Use only raw past measurements. Fixed descriptive slices are F/D, personal reference below 0.5 versus at least 0.5 points/GP, and identifiable role changes with adequate support. Describe missing families/slices explicitly.

## Evaluation and promotion

Report paired AUC gain with a player-cluster 95% resampling interval, AP versus prevalence, Brier, calibration and support, number of unique players and episodes. Alerts use the training scores' 90th percentile for each task (ties included); report issued volume, precision, recall and game lead to breach/confirmation or first/last target close. For B the target starts one appearance ahead and resolves five ahead; never claim five games' advance notice of a confirmed chart onset. Show actual false alarms and misses. Use training-standardized odds ratios or case-control contrasts with cluster uncertainty, describing associations only. Development minimum useful gain is +0.02 ROC AUC versus scoring, positive lower paired 95% bound, improved Brier and AP, and nontrivial alert coverage. A hot early should also exceed 0.60. Independent promotion additionally requires admissible provenance, a one-shot preauthorized later sample, useful calibration/alert performance and product release approval per target. An earlier threshold or a successful discrimination score alone cannot promote a target.
