## What was measured at each close

All game windows stay inside the same played-appearance segment. Level is the last five appearances' mean or pooled rate. Delta subtracts the immediately preceding 20-appearance window. Direction is the last two appearances minus the preceding three. The four process families use these three transforms for each measure. Individual inputs for pilot comparisons use the original five-minus-20 delta and unrounded ranks.

| Family | Raw measures and units |
|---|---|
| Deployment | All-strength, even-strength and power-play seconds per played game. PP fraction is PP seconds divided by the player's own total seconds across the window. Team power-play share and confirmed PP-unit membership require other evidence. |
| Generation | Shots on goal, misses, individual attempts, individual expected goals and individual high-danger chances per appearance. Individual xG/60 divides summed all-strength NST ixG by summed compatible NST ice seconds and multiplies by 3,600. |
| Conversion | Goals minus individual xG, primary and secondary assists per appearance. Personal shooting percentage uses 100 × (goals + 2)/(shots + 20), applying the same ten-percent prior in every window. On-ice finishing uses 100 × on-ice goals for / on-ice shots for, aggregated on the matched all-strength coverage. |
| Context | Calendar days since the previous played appearance less one, and whether the team changed from that appearance. Rest includes injury/selection gaps. A lineup or unit change needs an effective timestamp. |

The scoring ladder starts with training prevalence. The level model includes reference and scoring gap, which jointly determine the recent level and the distance to either fixed threshold. Persistence adds previous gap, current state age and previous hot/cold status. Age counts observed games only and can be a lower bound for a left-censored run. The last scoring stage includes the points about to leave the recent window at the next and following closes, and the two corresponding outgoing reference values. All these columns are read at issue. Future means, chart markers, number of games until a marker and phase membership never enter the design matrix.

Models use a median for partially missing numeric inputs, add training-observed missingness flags, discard training-constant columns, then center/scale each retained column. All three steps use the training rows alone. Study A restricts every fitted comparison to rows on which all five original indicator deltas exist. The pooled pilot replication preserves each original indicator's own support. In B, 25-game attempts and high-danger deltas are present on 4,427/9,857 training onset rows and 6,187/6,778 validation onset rows. This change in coverage limits transportability. The retained pilot file was materialized on 2025-09-26. The sources establish historical game observations with no immutable issue-time version.

Goals minus ixG, plus ixG, primary assists and secondary assists reconstruct points on common support. For example, the four five-game levels exactly account for recent scoring to numerical precision on all 8,021 forward validation games with all four values. The ridge penalty permits estimation under this dependence. Attributing that contribution to any one component requires care. The drop-family comparisons address incremental discrimination conditional on every other family.

## Reading the outputs

Each study has the same output layout. [Pilot results](evaluation/pilot/metrics.csv) · [Forward results](evaluation/forward/metrics.csv)

| Output | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| `metrics.csv` | One validation row per model, decision and side. AP is non-interpolated average precision. Its no-skill reference is event prevalence. Brier is the mean squared probability error. Calibration intercept and slope come from a logistic fit to held-out outcomes against score log-odds, with an intercept. A constant score has no defined slope. Prevalence alerts fire on every row because the frozen threshold includes ties. |
| `paired_gains.csv` | Challenger minus each scoring stage or the model missing one named family. Positive AUC/AP gain and negative Brier gain favor the challenger. The prespecified primary comparator is `scoring`. Each 95% percentile interval uses 600 draws of validation skaters with replacement, carrying all their rows and both predictions together. This preserves repeated-player, episode and overlapping-window dependence within a player. It conditions on fitted training models and covers one season. Interval multiplicity is unadjusted. |
| `coverage.csv`, `feature_coverage.csv` | Risk-set and follow-up attrition, crossing-boundary purges, train/validation support and observed values per feature. Episode counts at issue refer to already-active episodes. Upcoming chart episodes count distinct same-side chart confirmations reached by at least one eligible onset row within the study horizon, across all outcomes. A separate forward production block can satisfy the target without a chart episode. |
| `effects.csv`, `constant_fields.csv` | Training-event counts, odds ratio per training SD and approximate 95% player-cluster sandwich interval for each adjusted full-model coefficient. The penalized Hessian and player-summed estimating equations condition on the training transform. These are exploratory associations, with wide uncertainty for correlated features. The constants file identifies omitted fields for each fit. |
| `subgroups.csv`, `calibration.csv` | Counts and discrimination, AP, errors and alert metrics within prespecified slices. Subgroup performance cells require ten positives and ten negatives. Usage changed means an absolute 5-vs-20 difference of at least 60 PP seconds/GP or 120 total seconds/GP. This measures exposure, and line assignment remains unknown. Calibration bins use fixed intervals of width 0.1. |
| `validation_predictions.csv.gz`, `validation_features.csv.gz` | Paired scoring and challenger predictions, frozen training alert cutoffs and realized outcomes. Features are recorded once per unique issue appearance. Join by player ID, season and date, plus official game ID in B. Outcome dates and subsequent markers are post-issue fields. |
| `literal_pooled.csv` | Study A repeats 36 published pilot contrasts across hot/cold onset, continuation and exit with original individual support and higher-is-positive orientation. In held-out `metrics.csv`, `raw_auc_higher` preserves that orientation and `auc` follows the single-feature coefficient estimated on training. |

Study A early-onset positives have one game's lead to the first breach and two to confirmation. Its continuation and exit labels resolve one appearance later. Study B's forecast starts one appearance ahead and resolves at five, irrespective of when the chart transitions. `hits_with_chart_marker` counts true alerts that also exhibit the relevant chart state inside the horizon. Marker lead is conditional on observing such a marker. A continuation marker means the next observed same-side state and can precede a later exit. Recall measures recovered positive issue windows; nearby warnings can concern the same run.

## Pilot-source selection sensitivity

The post-score lineage audit found that pilot enrichment was absent at all 920 player-season terminal appearances. It was present at 723 of 888 penultimate appearances. A missingness indicator can therefore carry information about the extraction process and subsequent retention. Run `python3 lineage_sensitivity.py` after the primary evaluation. This diagnostic refits each B decision on the same training rows, dropping all missed-shot, attempt, high-danger and on-ice-finishing transforms and their missingness flags. It keeps independently matched full-season WGO inputs and NST individual xG/exposure. It measures the sensitivity to those four fields only. The original primary selection stands. [Full-season and pilot coverage](feature-availability.csv)

On forward hot onset the restricted model had AUC .639, paired gain +.089 versus scoring (95% CI +.046 to +.126), AP .201 and Brier .108. Its training upper-decile cutoff generated 532 alerts, 138 correct, for 25.9% precision and 16.1% recall. The identical numerical cutoff from the original primary generated 499 alerts, 128 correct. The other five decisions still fell short of the development criteria. The sensitivity was requested after seeing the validation results and has no independent qualification status. [All sensitivity metrics](evaluation/forward/lineage-sensitivity-metrics.csv) · [Paired intervals](evaluation/forward/lineage-sensitivity-gains.csv)

The research-only export freezes the scoring and primary challenger fits for forward hot onset. Both contain training medians, missingness flags, retained terms in design order, means, standard deviations, coefficients, intercept and the exact alert threshold. For each term apply its training transform, sum coefficient times standardized value plus intercept, and apply the logistic function. A score at or above the saved threshold fires. The JSON readback reproduced all 6,778 stored validation scores within 7 × 10⁻¹⁶ and all 561 scoring and 524 challenger alerts. Use the frozen predictors and issue-time measurement contract with the weights.
