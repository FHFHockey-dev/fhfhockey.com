Complete receipt is embedded in the manifest. No HTTP/DB call. This is not a claim about live records on the extra date.
- **Fix/test:** construct one shared inclusive calendar range from a single captured today, subtractingN−1; use the agreed schedule-date/time-zone policy rather than independently constructing instants. Extend the existing chart test with frozen boundary dates and route-admission fixture, including the extra oldest-date game and the correct first/last dates.
