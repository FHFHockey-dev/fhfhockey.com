Healthy reserves require explicit Yahoo injury-null and complete roster evidence; undefined injury and NA are excluded. Joint options maximize the number legally activated, then evaluate their combined gain, rather than summing mutually competing isolated gains. Weekly locks, shared swap partners and roster-order ties remain explicit. Existing tests include the nonadditive C/C/D counterexample. Lineup-gap reports use shared known-value drop ordering and preserve unknown players from automatic drops; reserve illegality can block an acquisition without pretending existing assignments are invalid.

The planner deduplicates identical forecasts and refuses conflicting duplicates. Future puck-drop eligibility, elapsed locked assignments, provider-out status, weekly ownership and fixed-slot occupancy are separate. Assignment, totals, comparison and conditional-tiebreak permissions remain independent. Unknown players contaminate their eligibility-connected slot set, including UTIL; schedule capacity can remain known while quality is unavailable. Points comparisons cancel only identical unavailable active plays. Categories pool components, retain a partial order, and disclose bounded heuristic search without claiming calibrated matchup probabilities or optimality. Goalie appearances and confirmed starts remain separate from expected starts. Team-game probability mass above one is refused. Expected minimum coverage never becomes credited starts.

Default search work limits depend deterministically on dates/catalog size; explicit time limits stop at completed checkpoints. Beam/frontier widths, candidate/fit limits and incomplete searches are disclosed. Reserve alternatives use their separate budget; original-roster unknowns are not automatic drops. Effective times use the latest equivalent pre-boundary minute and respect local-calendar/acquisition/waiver timing. Tiny independent assignment assertions and permutation tests are present. Large benchmark assertions were only read, not timed or run.
