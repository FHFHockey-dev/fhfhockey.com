## Findings

### AFULL-WTEST2-01 — P2, projected goalie coverage is labelled as an attained minimum

- **Test and claim:** `web/__tests__/components/RosterScheduleOptimizer/StreamingItinerary.test.tsx:69–80` constructs credited=1, confirmed=0, required=3, projected=3.2, risk=false and explicitly expects “meets G min” and “meets the minimum.” This protects a misleading reader label. It is a source-confirmed reader defect, not a missing-test finding.
- **Producer distinction:** `web/lib/rosterScheduleOptimizer/planning.ts:963–1002` computes `minimumAlreadyMet` from credited results, while `risk` compares the projected sum with the requirement. At `:1090` the result includes both `risk` and `minimumSatisfied`. These are different facts. The existing test fixture is compatible with the producer: `minimumSatisfied=false`, `risk=false` is possible.
- **Reader:** `web/components/RosterScheduleOptimizer/StreamingItinerary.tsx:25,33–44,111–115` uses `!evaluation.goalie.risk` to print attainment and attainment tradeoff wording, ignoring `minimumSatisfied`. `streamingChain.ts:103–108,371–373` also uses the projected-risk flag for its `met` field and chain preference. Preference for projected coverage is not itself proved wrong here; the unqualified attainment claim is.
- **Concrete proof:** `/tmp/astra-web-tests-2.cjs` extracts the exact evaluator declarations and exact reader helpers as AST nodes, transpiles them without imports and executes them in a VM. With credited=1, required=3 and projected=3.2, it obtains `{minimumAlreadyMet:false,risk:false}` and the exact string `Goalie starts 2/3 → 3.2/3 with this plan · meets the minimum`. Results are in `/tmp/astra-web-tests-2-results.json`. This is a pure helper execution, not React rendering; the existing rendering assertion at test line 76 is inspected source only.
- **Cross-reader inconsistency:** `RosterScheduleOptimizer.test.tsx:1499–1518` explicitly pins “Expected starts or appearances do not establish a satisfied minimum.” The corresponding `BenchDecisions.tsx:59–61` separates credited results and projected progress.
- **Correction:** retain the projected estimate and coverage preference, but label it as projected coverage. Use `minimumSatisfied`/credited evidence for actual attainment and test the false/false distinction above, plus already-credited attainment and unknown progress. Do not convert expected starts into recorded starts.
- **Reach/severity:** direct local component/producer source traced. No claim of current hosted exposure, provider data or live browser behavior. P2 reader-label/test-oracle defect; whole producer files remain pending their assigned audits.
