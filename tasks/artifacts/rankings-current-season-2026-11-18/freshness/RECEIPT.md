## Writer and scheduler constraints

- Both writers derive a regular-season player cohort from `wgo_skater_stats` played appearances through the requested date, using `(player_id, game_id)`. They read each player's exact latest `rolling_player_game_metrics` row for the requested strength and attach `rolling_player_metric_support_payloads` for that identity. The common snapshot label is the maximum eligible rolling game date and the freshness timestamp is a maximum `updated_at`. `as_of_date` caps inputs, so admission must explicitly confirm the resulting date equals the target. Player and team metadata support grouping/labels. By itself the WGO-to-rolling check can miss a whole omitted game and accepts absent support as unknown. Results Luck also reads rolling/support data.
