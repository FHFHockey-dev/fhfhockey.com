# Proposed use policy for the prepared native baseline

This is a model-use decision only. Database reads/writes, serving activation, deployment, recurring jobs and evaluation remain separately unapproved. It does not complete production acceptance.

The existing contribution store requires an owner-approved policy and independent review bound to each exact candidate. The FORGE owner confirmed that no existing approval covers these new methods. Its `prospectiveEvidenceRef` field requires a real reference but does not mechanically require performance scores. The proposed explicit interpretation is **genuine source availability and issuance for a future game before play**, with no claim of evaluated prospective accuracy, superiority or calibrated uncertainty. A string passing validation alone is insufficient evidence.

Also proposed are the three separately versioned roster-observation appearance/start methods, using the exact prepared input/receipt files and hashes in the inventory. They use a fresh official season-roster listing as an explicitly uncalibrated continuation assumption, not legal eligibility, health or a future confirmed lineup. Historical eligibility remains independently required. The current private preparation supports appearance probabilities for 14 of 19 owned players and separate start probabilities for both owned goalies across their next 20 scheduled team games. Five owned players remain withheld, covering 12 unresolved historical opportunities; a known injury conflict also applies to Fiala. No candidate participation probability is supplied by this decision.

Requested eventual uses are assignment, totals, comparison and conditional tie-breaking **only where the existing identity, participation, coverage, freshness, scoring and per-target permission checks pass**. Conditional ability alone cannot create expected points or certify a positive swap. Seven candidates have no verified appearance in the allocated sample, candidate goalie SO is missing, and PP role is unsupported. Missing quantities remain unknown. Goalie ratios retain ratio-of-sums estimands and compatible denominators.

After a positive decision, preserve every original preview and create new issuance versions using actual approval/issuance/review times. Do not backdate approval, replace source capture clocks, extend original source validity or reuse expired canary authority. Independent review must bind the final policy hash and exact final bundle checksum. A separate, concrete publication package will enumerate the immutable rows, exact hashes, request/write limits, target project, readback and rollback before any live execution or activation.
