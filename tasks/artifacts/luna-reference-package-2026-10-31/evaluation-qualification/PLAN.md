## What the human labels

- **Identity, per occurrence:** supplied occurrence ID and exact source span; allowed canonical candidate or explicit ambiguous/missing/conflicting/not-player/needs-widening status; dated evidence reference and why alternatives cannot be resolved. Mark completeness; do not infer a label for a skipped occurrence.
- **Goalie wording, per occurrence:** confirmed/ruled-out/likely/projected/unknown or omission permitted by the existing schema, independent of identity confidence. First-off is projected; practice/warmup attendance alone is not a start; explicit warmup starter wording may support confirmed wording with the existing downstream holds. Preserve negation, conditions, chronology, corrections and mixed subjects. Broader injury/PP/lineup information remains out of scope.
- **Evidence:** independently choose allowed exact excerpts including subject and governing qualifiers; derive UTF-16 `[start,end)` mechanically from retained original bytes and verify them by slicing. Freeze semantically equivalent permitted variants before predictions. Repeated exact quotes remain explicitly ambiguous. A coordinate candidate is never a substitute for the raw result.
- **Decisions:** label the actual pre-bound statement and question, not a model-created paraphrase or world outcome. Record relevance, `supported`/`contradicted`/`insufficient_evidence`, and the existing wording enum. Use the pinned mapper/reference/scoring behavior without importing thresholds from older comparison experiments. No invented probability target or calibration claim.
