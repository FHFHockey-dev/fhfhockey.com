## Review library and cadence proposal

Each review artifact has original lineage, text/packet version, proposals, request IDs and holds. Review uncertainty first by identity ambiguity, missing source/game/time, contradiction/retraction and provider/budget failure. Keep rejected/unresolved cases alongside successful *adjudicated source interpretations*. New examples require recorded human reviewer/date, original source hash/lineage, exact claim/subject evidence, applicable policy/extraction versions and explicit unresolved canonical/game fields. Prompt examples/rules are versioned changes followed by the finite development checks; they do not automatically retrain Luna. Keep an untouched, lineage-separated holdout for later qualification.

Propose a bounded weekly review of up to20 uncertain cases plus a small sample of agreed results, preserving disagreements. Consider monthly only after actual queue volume and error severity justify it. Parent must verify queue/source/cadence and confirm setup before any automation is created. None was created here.

Remaining blockers are activation inputs/authoritative shared budget and release coordination, not a denied operation. Current implementation does not reduce alias emails, fix historical homepage rows, execute replacements, publish news or establish representative Luna accuracy.
