## Metric/source boundary

NHL gameLog goals/assists/points/shots and mm:ss TOI are all-strength regular-season observations; pooled TOI is seconds and output min/GP or events/60. Existing registry NST counts.ixg is individual xG; counts_oi.xgf/xga are on-ice xG; selected strength/model/revision/exposure must match. BSH sums same-game BLK + SOG + HITS; absent component is missing, not zero. PP time cannot exceed all-strength TOI. xG% pools xGF/(xGF+xGA); zero denominator is undefined. A complete season supports recent/STD overlap; missing rows never become a short season. No new source reads.
