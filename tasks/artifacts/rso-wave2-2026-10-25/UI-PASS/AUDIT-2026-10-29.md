# RSO visual pass: clutter audit (2026-10-29)

Read-only look at TJ's live RSO tab (Yahoo league, 2,056 px wide desktop). No names or screenshots are kept. The figures come from the rendered DOM.

## Measurements

- **Controls:** 197 visible buttons and 71 visible inputs on one screen.
- **Text styles:** 25 size/weight combinations. 9–10 px text covers about 440 elements (9/400, 9/700, 10/400, 10/700), and 11 px about 440 more. 16 text colors.
- **Corner radii:** six in use (3, 4, 5, 6 and 10 px, plus 50%).
- **Status messages:** three shown at once ("Provider inputs refreshed…", "Rankings appear once…", "Preserve this exact working context…") alongside the Data health pill.

## Where the clutter is

| Area | What competes | Proposed direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Top bar** | About 11 controls in one row: connection status, synced/adds-left, date range, Current week, search, refresh-status pill, Data health pill, Undo, Matchup, Save to account, Refresh provider, ☰ | Group into three clusters: context (league · week · adds left), status (one combined health/refresh indicator), actions (Save plus an overflow for Undo, Matchup, Refresh). Transient success messages become a toast that fades. |
| **Top five team schedules** | A full-width strip above the work area | Collapse to a slim row, or move it into the planner as a tab. Keep the score and GP/OFF chips. |
| **Current Roster** | A six-column table; inline DROP chip with a two-line caption ("Ownership unknown…"); three stat cards; a compare box; goalie starts | Move the caution text to a tooltip or icon. Merge the stat cards and compare box into one compact summary row. Keep Protect and More, but quieter. |
| **Optimized Week** | Two objective toggles, Optimize lineup, four view tabs, the slider, and a 7-day grid with many dashed "Empty" cells | Render empty slots as faint placeholders without the word "Empty". Mute past days further. Put the objective and view switches in one control row. |
| **Best Fit** | Confirm prompt, two sort buttons, a status line, Find & filter, cards (plus alternative lines, now being tidied) | Fold the confirm prompt into one page-level "Confirm inputs" bar. Give each status line one consistent slot. Show sort and filter as a single toolbar. |
| **Type and shape** | 25 text styles, 16 colors, 6 radii | Reduce to a small scale (for example 11/12/14/16/20 px; two weights), cut the colors to about 6 role tokens, and use 2 radii (small and card). Use existing `vars.scss` tokens where they exist. |

## Constraints for any change

- **Behavior:** no change to planning results, gates or protections.
- **Layout and styles:** keep the page at 90% max width, centred (`$page-max-width`). CSS-module selectors stay pure (each includes a local class), checked with the pure-check script.
- **Phone width:** keep or improve it; the compact cards from #397 must still fit.
- **Overlapping sessions:** two sessions are editing RSO files now. `rso-alt-tidy` has `CandidateBrowser` and `StreamingItinerary`; `rso-recent-form` has data files only. The visual pass starts after `rso-alt-tidy` is committed, or takes over its files.

## TJ decisions (pop-up, 2026-10-29)

- **Scope:** declutter in place (option 1), keeping the three-column layout.
- **Easy to revert:** each step is its own local commit, so any step can be reverted alone.
- **Step by step:** after each major targeted change, an approve/edit pop-up with before/after screenshots. The screenshots come from local fixture data, never the live league.
- **Plugins:** TJ installed frontend-design, Design (critique, ux-copy, design-system, accessibility-review) and Axe Accessibility, all enabled on the account. The session uses them; if Axe needs credentials or setup, it asks TJ rather than entering anything itself.
- **Start:** after `rso-alt-tidy` is verified and committed.
